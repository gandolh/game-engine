Hollow is the third game — and the strongest test that the engine is really
generic. It's a **director/observer multi-generational social simulation**: no
player avatar. You author personas, seed a small town, then observe and perturb
it as it lives, works, reproduces, quarrels and dies across generations.

## The thesis: decisions must have weight

Hollow grew out of an earlier LLM agent-society experiment where unanchored
agents simply hallucinated their lives. The corrective premise here: **every
choice must be anchored** — by needs (`food` / `rest` / `wealth` / `belonging`),
by scarce *located* resources, and by a real economy. Scarcity, in turn,
regulates population. The BDI substrate is the same one Farm Valley uses,
promoted into `@engine/core/agent` so both games share it — which makes the two
games *more* isolated, not less.

## What's built

Built via a plan-split-dispatch orchestration (an Opus controller, Sonnet
executors), one brief per wave. All of it is on `main`.

<Steps>

1. **M1 — the headless sim ✅** (`hollow-01…07`) — workspace skeleton; engine
   agent-kernel promotion + a Farm refactor onto it; needs / economy / scarcity;
   relationships & emergent communities; lifecycle, pair-bonding & genetics;
   social verbs; the headless CLI + export. The **M1 exit bar passed** 2026-07-20.

2. **M2 — the 3D layer ✅** (`hollow-08…09`) — a true-3D renderer promoted into
   `@engine/core/render3d`, plus the gene-driven cozy town scene. Built first on
   WebGPU, **ported to WebGL2 on 2026-08-18** when WebGL2 became the repo's only
   backend; the live image was re-verified in-sandbox 2026-09-19 and is no longer
   Chrome-gated.

3. **M3 — research surfaces ✅** (`hollow-10…11`) — the event chronicle +
   dashboard, persona authoring, shocks and replay. The client and the headless
   CLI share one set of `observe` serializers, so a metric can't mean two things.

4. **M4 — governance & antagonism ✅** (`hollow-12`) — emergent leadership, job
   assignment, feuds and sabotage arcs.

5. **M5 — daily life & mortality ✅** (`hollow-14`, `hollow-15`) — leader-assigned
   jobs, a diurnal routine around one central hearth, rare private interactions;
   then starvation death, persistent corpses, graveyard burial by a grave-digger,
   rot → disease, and a medic.

</Steps>

## The LLM seam, and the number that justifies it

`hollow-13` added the bounded LLM **rationalizer**: it may only *choose among*
the feasible actions BDI already generated, and narrate why. It cannot invent an
action, it is **off by default**, and with it off the sim is byte-identical.

The interesting part is that the seam was *measured* rather than asserted
(`hollow-16`). Under a contrarian diagnostic, **~12% of answers are adopted** —
low-rate but genuinely live, and when an answer is adopted the world provably
diverges. Every run now prints its own adoption rate, so the seam can't quietly
become decorative. One spec remains queued: moving the attachment point to a
decision whose subject outlives the ~40-tick answer lag.

<Aside type="caution" title="A war story worth keeping">
  Lifecycle first showed population **bistability** — food scarcity alone couldn't
  bound it. The fix was a **density-dependent birth brake**
  (`BIRTH_PERCAPITA_FOOD_TARGET`), giving a bounded, never-extinct ~15–65 plateau
  across seeds. Instrument the real run; re-derive the premise.
</Aside>
